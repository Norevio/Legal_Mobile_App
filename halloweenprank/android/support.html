Frequently asked questions

Does the App make real calls or send real messages?

No. Everything is a fake, fictional simulation made for fun pranks. No real call, message, or notification is sent to anyone.

Why don't some videos play right away?

Some prank videos are downloaded on first use. Connect to the internet and give the App a moment to finish downloading, then try again.

The camera preview doesn't show during a video call.

Allow camera access for the App in Android Settings. The preview is shown only on your screen and is never recorded or uploaded.

I'm not receiving Halloween alerts.

Turn on Halloween Alerts in the App's Settings and allow notifications for the App in Android Settings. On Android, also allow exact alarms and disable battery restrictions for the App if alerts arrive late.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings → Language in the App and choose your language.

How do I set a wallpaper?

Open the Wallpaper tab, pick an image, and tap Set Wallpaper.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall the App to remove all local data.
