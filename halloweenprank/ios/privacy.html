Halloween Call: Monster Prank ("the App", "we", "us") is an entertainment app for iOS that lets you play fake spooky video calls, chats, and sounds, set Halloween wallpapers, and use a Halloween countdown widget, to prank your friends. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

The App does not require an account, and we do not operate servers that store your personal data.

1. Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not require sign-in, email, phone number, or any account.
  • We do not record, store, or upload camera images, audio, or your photos.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

2. Information stored on your device

Your settings (such as language, alert and sound preferences), downloaded prank videos, and your countdown widget configuration are stored locally in the App's private storage. You can remove them by uninstalling the App.

4. Permissions

  • Camera — used only to show your own live preview during a prank video call. Camera images are not recorded, stored, or uploaded.
  • Notifications — used only to deliver the Halloween alerts and fake notifications you enable. You can turn them off in the App or in iOS Settings.

  • Tracking — used only to request your permission for personalized advertising.

5. Data security

Data created by the App is kept in the App's private, sandboxed storage on your device. Network requests are made over secure connections where supported.

6. Children

The App is an entertainment app with spooky (Halloween) themed content and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App shows advertising; we do not knowingly direct ads to children.

7. Your choices

  • Limit ad tracking or reset your Advertising Identifier in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking / Apple Advertising.
  • Turn off alerts and sound effects in the App's Settings.
  • Uninstall the App to remove all local data.
