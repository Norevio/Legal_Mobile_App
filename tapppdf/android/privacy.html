Privacy Terms Support

TApp PDF ("the App", "we", "us") is a PDF utility for Android that scans, recognizes, merges, splits, compresses, views, edits, and signs PDF documents. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

We designed TApp PDF to be local-first: your documents are processed directly on your device and are not uploaded to us. We do not operate servers that store your files, and we do not require an account to use the App.

1. Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not require sign-in, email, or any account.
  • We do not upload, store, or have access to your PDFs, scanned images, or recognized (OCR) text — these stay on your device.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

2. Information processed on your device

Documents & scans: PDFs you import, photos/scans you capture, and text produced by OCR are processed entirely on your device.

Files & history are stored locally in the App's private storage so the App can show your recent files and activity. You can delete them anytime in the App, or by removing the App.

3. Third-party services we use

ServiceWhat it does
Google AdMob (advertising)If you have not purchased an ad-free option, the App shows ads via AdMob, which may collect device identifiers (including the Google Advertising ID) and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads. Where required, the App requests consent via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP); if you decline personalization, ads are still shown but are not personalized.

4. Permissions

  • Camera — used only to scan documents into PDFs. Images stay on device.
  • Photos / media — used only to import images you select for OCR or PDF creation. We do not browse or upload your library.

5. Data security

Files created by the App are kept in the App's private, sandboxed storage on your device and are not shared unless you explicitly export or share them.

6. Children

TApp PDF is a general-audience productivity app and is not primarily directed to children. It is not intended for use by children under 13. The App shows advertising; we do not knowingly direct ads to children or collect data from children.

7. Your choices

  • Disable ads by purchasing "Remove Ads" or "Lifetime".
  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Delete files and history in the App, or uninstall to remove all local data.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes are reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
