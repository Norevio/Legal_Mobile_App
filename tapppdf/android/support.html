Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. TApp PDF is local-first — scanning, OCR, merging, splitting, compressing, and editing all happen on your device. Your files are not sent to our servers.

Why didn't my text-only PDF get smaller after compression?

Compression re-renders pages as images, which works best for scanned or image-heavy PDFs. A PDF that is mostly vector text may not shrink — that's expected.

How do I remove ads?

Open the Premium screen and buy "Remove Ads" (one-time) or "Lifetime". Already purchased? Tap "Restore Purchases".

I bought Premium but it's not active on my new device.

Open the Premium screen and tap "Restore Purchases" while signed in with the same Google account used for the purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, select TApp PDF, and cancel there.

How accurate is OCR?

OCR uses on-device text recognition (ML Kit). Accuracy depends on image quality, lighting, and language. For best results, use clear, well-lit, high-contrast scans.

How do I delete my data?

Delete files and history inside the App, or uninstall the App to remove all local data.
